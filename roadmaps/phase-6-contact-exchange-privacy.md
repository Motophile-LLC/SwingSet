# Phase 6 — Contact Exchange and Privacy

## Goal
Handle mutual exchange of permitted information without chat, and keep it privacy-safe.

## Core rules
- Contact is mutual
- Attendees choose:
  - what info is public
  - what info is shared only after tap verification
- No in-app messaging
- After mutual verified meet, attendees can share permitted contact info and/or links to profiles

## Photo + recognition
- Event-day photo is required at event start
- Profiles are dormant after the event

## Consent + gating
- Mutual agreement is required before exchange unlocks
- Host may target prompts, but the exchange still depends on attendee preferences and verification.

## Phase 6 open decisions
- exact consent UX for unlock
- which contact fields are supported in v1
- server storage vs pass-through behavior
